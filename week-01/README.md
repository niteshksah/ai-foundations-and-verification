# Week 01 — AI Foundations and Verification

## 1. Learning Objectives

This week, I am learning the basic concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Deep Learning (DL), Generative AI, and AI Agents. I am also learning how to verify AI-generated information instead of trusting every answer automatically.

## 2. Assignment Questions

I will answer all ten questions using the **A-E-V-R method**:

- **A — Answer:** Explain the concept in my own words.
- **E — Evidence:** Provide reliable sources, examples, or experiment results.
- **V — Verification:** Check important claims against trustworthy references or practical tests.
- **R — Reflection:** Explain what I learned and what I would do differently.

## 5. Verification Principles

1. I will not assume that a fluent AI response is necessarily correct.
2. I will check important factual claims against reliable sources.
3. I will record the sources and practical tests I actually use.
4. I will distinguish verified facts from uncertain or unsupported claims.
5. I will disclose my use of AI tools honestly.

## 6. AI Usage Declaration
