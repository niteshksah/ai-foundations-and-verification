## Lessons Learned

- A confident AI response is not sufficient evidence by itself.
- Official manufacturer documentation is useful for checking technical specifications.
- Correct answers can still have weak or unclear source attribution.
- I should record actual results and avoid claiming that an error occurred when I did not find one.
